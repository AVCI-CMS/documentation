# Notification Preferences

**Route:** `(merchant) > /merchant/settings/notification`

The Notifications Settings page allows merchants to manage which notifications they receive and through which channels (Email, SMS, Push, In-App).

## Channel Configuration

You have full control over the notification channels for various events related to your store operations. If you disable a channel for a specific event, you will no longer receive notifications through that medium.

- **Email:** Important order summaries and daily reports.
- **SMS:** Time-sensitive alerts, such as immediate low stock warnings or payout failures.
- **In-App:** Alerts delivered directly within the AVCI CMS dashboard.
- **Push:** Mobile app notifications (if configured).

### Managing Categories

Notifications are grouped into distinct categories for easier management:

- **Order Notifications:** Receive alerts when a new order is placed, fulfilled, or canceled.
- **Financial Notifications:** Get updates regarding payouts, early withdrawal approvals, and billing.
- **General Notifications:** General alerts about platform updates or system status.
- **Comment Notifications:** Alerts when a user comments on your product or brand profile.
- **Support Tickets:** Notifications regarding the status of your open support requests.

## Overrides & Global Policies

:::info Admin Permissions
By default, the Workspace Administrator determines the global availability of notification channels. You can only configure channels that have been explicitly enabled at the workspace level. If a channel (e.g., SMS) is disabled globally by the administrator, it will not appear as an option in your notification preferences.
:::

## How to Disable a Channel

1. Navigate to **Settings > Notifications** in your Merchant Dashboard.
2. Select the relevant category tab (e.g., *Order Notifications*).
3. Find the specific notification event you wish to modify.
4. Uncheck the box for the channel (Email, SMS, etc.) you want to disable.
5. Click the **Save** button to apply your changes.
