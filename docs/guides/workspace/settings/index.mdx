# Workspace Settings

**Menu Path:** `Workspace > Settings`

:::info Permission Requirements
To view or modify workspace settings, you must have the **Workspace:Settings:View** or **Workspace:Manage** permission.
:::

The **Workspace Settings** module gives workspace administrators control over global business rules, visual branding, sales tax policies, catalog behaviors, and team access levels across the entire platform.
