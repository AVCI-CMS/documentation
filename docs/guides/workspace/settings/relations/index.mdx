# Entity & Relation Settings

**Menu Path:** `Workspace > Settings > Relations`

:::info Permission Requirements
To configure entity relation rules, you must have the **Workspace:Manage** or **Workspace:Settings:View** permission.
:::

The **Relation Settings** interface controls the operational parameters, onboarding requirements, and verification workflows applied to external partners across your workspace: Merchants, Brands, Organizations, Places, and End-Users.
