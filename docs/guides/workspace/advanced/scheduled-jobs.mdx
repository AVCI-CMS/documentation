# Scheduled Background Jobs

**Menu Path:** `Workspace > Advanced > Scheduled Jobs`

:::info Permission Requirements
Viewing scheduled jobs requires the **Workspace:Manage** permission.
:::

The **Scheduled Jobs** interface provides real-time visibility into the automated background processes (cron jobs) running for your workspace. These background workers ensure automated tasks execute reliably without degrading storefront response times.

## Core Automated Tasks

* **Exchange Rate Synchronization:** Automatically refreshes multi-currency foreign exchange rates twice daily.
* **Abandoned Cart Reminders:** Dispatches reminder emails to customers who left items in their checkout baskets after a configured inactivity window.
* **Inventory Depletion Sweeps:** Automatically marks out-of-stock products as hidden or triggers restocking notifications.
* **Settlement Calculations:** Aggregates merchant commission balances at the end of each billing cycle.

## Manual Execution
If you urgently need updated currency rates or need to trigger an inventory re-indexing ahead of a flash sale, click **"Run Now"** next to the corresponding job.
