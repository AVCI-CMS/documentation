# Legal Agreements & Compliance

**Menu Path:** `Workspace > Advanced > Agreements`

:::info Permission Requirements
Managing legal agreements requires the **Workspace Owner** or **Workspace:Manage** permission.
:::

The **Agreements** module allows you to define, version, and mandate legal contracts, privacy statements, and terms of service across your storefront and vendor networks.

## Supported Agreement Types

1. **Terms of Service (ToS):** Governing rules for storefront visitors and end-customers.
2. **Privacy Policy & Data Protection (KVKK / GDPR):** Disclosures detailing personal data handling, cookies, and customer rights.
3. **Vendor Marketplace Agreements:** Mandatory contracts presented to new Merchants and Brands during the onboarding wizard before they are authorized to list products or sell tickets.
4. **Distance Sales Agreement & Cancellation Policy:** Legally required e-commerce disclosures automatically appended to checkout receipts and order confirmation emails.

## Versioning & Consent Tracking
When you publish a revised version of an agreement, you can require users to re-consent on their next login or purchase. Consent timestamps and IP records are stored for regulatory compliance audits.
