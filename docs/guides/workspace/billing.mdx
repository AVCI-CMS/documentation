# Billing & Invoices

**Menu Path:** `Workspace > Billing`

:::info Permission Requirements
Managing payment methods and downloading invoices requires the **Workspace Owner** role or **Workspace:Billing:Manage** permission.
:::

The **Billing & Invoices** module handles all subscription payments, stored credit cards, corporate tax information, and official billing receipts for your AVCI CMS account.

---

## Managing Payment Methods

You can store multiple payment cards on file, designating one as the default for recurring subscription renewals.

### Adding a Credit or Debit Card
1. Go to **Workspace > Billing**.
2. In the **Payment Methods** section, click **"Add Payment Method"**.
3. Enter the cardholder name, 16-digit card number, expiry date (MM/YY), and CVV/CVC code.
4. (Optional) Check **"Set as default payment method"**.
5. Click **"Save Card"**. 

:::note Security & Compliance
AVCI CMS does not store complete card numbers on its servers. All card details are tokenized and processed via certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment partners (e.g. Stripe).
:::

### Updating or Removing Cards
*   To set an alternate card as primary, click the three-dots menu (`...`) next to the card and select **"Set as Default"**.
*   To remove an expired or unused card, select **"Remove"**. Note: You cannot remove the default payment method if an active paid plan is in place; assign a new default card first.

---

## Invoices & Receipts

Whenever your subscription renews or plan adjustments are processed, an official tax invoice is generated automatically.

*   **Invoice History Table:** Displays invoice number, billing date, amount charged, currency, payment status, and a direct download link.
*   **Downloading PDF:** Click the PDF icon next to any invoice to download an e-archive compliant tax invoice for your accounting records.
*   **Billing Contact Details:** Ensure your legal company name, Tax Office (*Vergi Dairesi*), Tax Identification Number (*VKN / TCKN*), and registered corporate address are up to date under **Billing Information** to ensure valid invoice generation.
