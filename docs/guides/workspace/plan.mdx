# Subscription Plans

**Menu Path:** `Workspace > Plan`

:::info Permission Requirements
Managing or upgrading subscription plans requires the **Workspace Owner** role or **Workspace:Billing:Manage** permission.
:::

AVCI CMS operates on tiered subscription plans designed to scale with your business volume—from growing independent merchants to multi-national enterprises with high-throughput API demands.

---

## Plan Tiers Comparison

The platform provides three core subscription tiers:

| Feature / Limit | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Active Products** | Up to 1,000 | Up to 25,000 | Unlimited |
| **Monthly API Calls** | 100,000 requests | 2,500,000 requests | Custom / Dedicated |
| **Media Storage** | 10 GB | 100 GB | Dedicated Cloud Bucket |
| **Bandwidth** | 50 GB / mo | 500 GB / mo | Unlimited |
| **Team Member Seats** | 3 seats | 15 seats | Unlimited |
| **Custom Apex & Subdomains** | 1 domain | 5 domains | Unlimited |
| **Automated SSL Certificates** | Included | Included | Custom Cert Upload |
| **Support SLA** | Community / Email | Priority 24/7 Support | Dedicated Account Manager |

## Upgrading or Downgrading

To modify your active subscription:

1. Navigate to **Workspace > Plan**.
2. Review the plan comparison matrix. Your current active tier is indicated with a **"Current Plan"** badge.
3. Select your preferred billing frequency:
   *   **Monthly Billing:** Flexible, cancel anytime.
   *   **Annual Billing:** Includes a discounted rate (typically 20% savings).
4. Click **"Upgrade Plan"** or **"Switch Plan"** on the target tier.
5. If upgrading, the prorated difference will be calculated automatically and charged to your primary payment method.

:::tip Proration Policy
When upgrading mid-cycle, unused days from your current tier are automatically credited towards the new plan invoice.
:::

---

## Canceling or Pausing

If you need to suspend your subscription:
*   Downgrades or cancellations take effect at the end of the current paid billing cycle.
*   Your data remains securely archived in read-only mode for 60 days following plan cancellation, allowing you to reactivate without data loss.
