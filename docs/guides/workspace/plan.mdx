# Subscription Plans & Feature Matrix

**Menu Path:** `Workspace > Plan`

:::info Permission Requirements
Managing or upgrading subscription plans requires the **Workspace Owner** (`WORKSPACE_OWNER`) role or `workspace:manage` permission.
:::

AVCI CMS offers four customer subscription tiers — **Basic**, **Pro**, **Business**, and **Enterprise** — designed to scale from personal/showcase projects to high-volume e-commerce stores and multi-vendor marketplaces.

:::note Sales & E-Commerce Boundary
The **Pro** plan is built for content, catalog showcase, team collaboration, custom domains, caching, and dynamic agreements — it does **not** include online sales or checkout modules. Tenants that require e-commerce and sales workflows should choose **Business** (or **Enterprise** for multi-vendor marketplaces).
:::

## Upgrading or Switching Plans

1. Navigate to **Workspace > Plan**.
2. Review the current plan badge and resource quotas.
3. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual** billing.
4. Click **Upgrade Plan** to complete checkout via Polar.sh or your configured billing provider.
5. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current paid billing cycle.
