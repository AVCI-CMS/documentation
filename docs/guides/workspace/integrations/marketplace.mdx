# App Marketplace

**Menu Path:** `Workspace > Integrations > App Marketplace`

:::info Permission Requirements
Installing or configuring third-party integrations requires the **Workspace:Integrations:Manage** permission.
:::

The **App Marketplace** is a 1-click extension catalog that connects AVCI CMS to leading cloud software without requiring custom code.

---

## Featured Categories

### 1. Payment Providers
*   **Stripe:** Global credit card checkout, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and localized European payment methods.
*   **Iyzico:** Turkey's premier payment gateway supporting installments (*taksit*), 3D Secure, and marketplace sub-merchant split payouts.

### 2. Shipping & Carrier Couriers
*   **Domestic & Global Couriers:** Direct API integration with Yurtiçi Kargo, Aras Kargo, MNG Kargo, Kolay Gelsin, PTT, DHL, and UPS.
*   **Automated Tracking:** Automatically generates shipment tracking URLs and syncs delivery status back to buyer accounts.

### 3. Accounting & ERP Sync
*   **Automatic Invoicing:** Sync orders to platforms like Logo, Netsis, Paraşüt, and Xero upon payment completion.

### 4. Marketing & Analytics
*   **Google Analytics 4 & Tag Manager:** One-click integration with your Measurement ID for e-commerce conversion tracking.
*   **Meta (Facebook) Pixel:** Server-side and client-side conversion tracking for retargeting campaigns.

---

## Installing an App

1. Browse the App Marketplace to locate your desired provider.
2. Click **"Connect"** or **"Install"**.
3. Enter your provider API credentials (e.g. API Secret Key, Merchant Token).
4. Toggle status to **Active** and click **Save Changes**.
