# App Integrations & Marketplace

**Menu Path:** `Workspace > Integrations`

:::info Permission Requirements
Managing integrations requires the **Workspace:Manage** or **Workspace:Integrations:Manage** permission.
:::

The **Integrations Hub** connects your AVCI CMS workspace with external services—including domestic and international payment gateways, shipping couriers, accounting/ERP suites, customer support tools, and marketing pixels.
